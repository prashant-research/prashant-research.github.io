Variables and multiple Y-axes

Add up to three additional Y variables. Use secondary/right-offset axes or separate panels for different units.

For contour and 3D plots.
Multiple Y-axes can be difficult to interpret. Separate panels are usually clearer when quantities have unrelated units or scales.

Labels, title and legend

Use automatic, custom or no labels. Matplotlib math text such as $\Delta G$ / eV is accepted in publication mode.

Version 1.0 Notes

Most common plots work in publication and interactive modes. Pair and joint plots are publication-mode features. Matplotlib uses scattered-data triangulation for contour plots, while interactive contour exploration uses colour-mapped points unless the data are already gridded.

Multiple Y-axes are available, but separate panels are often clearer for unrelated units.
